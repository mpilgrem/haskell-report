\section{Representations of C types}
\subsection{Integral types}
These types are are represented as \haddocktt{newtype}s of types in \haddocktt{Data.Int}
 and \haddocktt{Data.Word}, and are instances of \haddocktt{Eq}, \haddocktt{Ord}, \haddocktt{Num}, \haddocktt{Read}, \haddocktt{Show},
 \haddocktt{Enum}, \haddocktt{Storable}, \haddocktt{Bounded}, \haddocktt{Real}, \haddocktt{Integral} and \haddocktt{Bits}.\par
